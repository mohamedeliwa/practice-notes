# CURB 65 score

CURB 65 is an evidence based tool used to risk stratify patients presenting with pneumonia.
CURB 65 is validated to predict 30-day mortality in CAP

| Criterion                                                      | Marker |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Confusion                                                      | 1      |
| urea > 7 mmol/L (≈ 42 mg/dL)                                   | 1      |
| Respiration rate >= 30/min                                     | 1      |
| Blood pressure: systolic <= 90 mmHg and/or diastolic <= 60mmHg | 1      |
| Aged >= 65 years                                               | 1      |

- score of 0 or 1: low risk (consider home-based care)
- score of 2: intermediate risk (consider hospital-based care or hospital supervised outpatient treatment)
- 3 or more: high risk (consider ICU-based care).

Note:

- The CURB65 derivative CRB65 does not rely on laboratory blood tests and may be used in the community to help assess which patients require hospital admission.
- Confusion defined as AMTS ≤8, or new disorientation in person, place or time
